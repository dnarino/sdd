Clarify the spec in $ARGUMENTS (if empty, use the most recent folder in `specs/`).

1. Read its `spec.md` and look for anything unclear, missing, or untestable, especially missing `IF … THEN` error cases.
2. Add any new doubts to "Open questions".
3. Ask me the unanswered questions **one at a time** and wait for each answer.
4. After each answer: mark the question `- [x]`, write the answer, and turn it into a new or clearer requirement or an "Out of scope" item.
5. Stop when every question is answered. Don't write the plan.
